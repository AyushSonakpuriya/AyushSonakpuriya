# 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Ayush Sonakpuriya**, an Information Technology engineering student focused on building software at the intersection of **clean UI/UX, logical engineering, and real-world problem solving**.

I enjoy taking an idea from a rough concept to something people can actually interact with. My current interests revolve around **full-stack web development, UI/UX engineering, product thinking, AI-assisted development, and developer communities**.

Rather than treating design and engineering as separate disciplines, I like working where the two overlap.

> **Good interfaces should feel simple. Good systems should behave predictably. Good products need both.**

Currently pursuing **B.Tech in Information Technology at Chameli Devi Group of Institutions, Indore**, while building projects, participating in developer communities, organizing technical events, and exploring modern development workflows.
